Case Study · 02

Quote Wizard Redesign - fixing our main flow

Background

Azami Global is an intellectual property law firm that files and manages patents and trademarks for clients across 170+ jurisdictions. Their paralegals coordinate this work daily, requesting filings and assigning associate firms to handle them in each region.

iPeer is the platform Azami Global runs this work through, handling case management, filings, and associate coordination in one system. Its "Create New Quote" flow is how paralegals request a new filing and pick the associate firms to do the work - the highest-stakes, most frequent task in the product.

Role
Lead Product Designer
Timeline
3 months
Team
1 designer, 1 PM, 2 engineers
Status
Live with measured impact

Why - The Problem

40% of Quotes Abandoned

Quoting is the highest-stakes, most frequent task in iPeer - every filing starts here. But finishing the wizard didn't actually finish the task: sending instructions to associates required a second, disconnected step that re-asked questions already answered, and the total cost stayed hidden until the very last step. Once the real cost finally appeared - too late for paralegals to adjust scope - close to 40% of quotes were abandoned at that last step.

*This is the old version - after the last step in the quote wizard, opens a new popup with more steps.

The Approach

Shorter By Design, Filled In By AI

Merged quote creation and instructing associates into one five-step flow instead of a wizard plus a disconnected modal - no functionality lost, no question asked twice. AI auto-fills application details and suggests filing regions from the patent number entered up front, so the flow only asks for what actually needs a human decision. Cost is visible starting at region selection instead of the final step.

One Continuous Flow

Two disconnected steps merged into a single five-step wizard.

AI-Suggested Everything

Regions and application details suggested automatically from the patent number.

Cost Visible Early

Price shown as a range from the very first step, not hidden until the end.

Pre-Filled, Not Re-Typed

Application details and regions arrive filled in, ready to review.

Collapsible, Scannable Sections

Long forms broken into sections that show their own completion status.

Side-by-Side Comparison

Associates compared on rating, price, specialty, and track record before picking one.

The Final Design

Step 1 - Services

Fewer Steps, Cost Visible From Day One

We merged two separate steps into one, cutting the number of clicks needed to start a quote. The price now shows as a range from the very first step, instead of staying hidden until the end of the flow.

Step 2 - Regions

AI-Suggested Regions

Instead of manually searching and adding each country, the system now suggests filing regions automatically based on the patent number entered earlier in the flow, flagging them with an "AI Suggested" badge.

Step 3 - Details

New Collapsible Sections

Fields that used to live together on one long page are now organized into collapsible sections, each with its own completion status — so users always know exactly how much is left.

Step 4 - Associates

Comparison-Driven Selection

Each region's associate is chosen by comparing rating, price, specialty match, and track record side-by-side, instead of picking blind, so paralegals can see exactly why one firm is recommended over another.

Step 5 - Review & Send

Error Prevention

Before sending, users now see a full summary - application details, regions, associates, and total cost - with a chance to attach documents and catch mistakes before instructions go out.

Confirmation Screen

Visibility & Control After Sending

A vanishing toast with an automatic redirect is now a persistent confirmation with a case number per region, and the user choosing the next step.
