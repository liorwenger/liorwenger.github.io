Context

Building Wix's First Native Reviews Product

Store owners on Wix were stuck using third-party review apps that looked off-brand, broke on mobile, and took hours to configure. The gap was obvious - Wix needed its own reviews product, built to feel native across millions of different stores. This project was the answer.

The Problem

Third-Party Apps Weren't Good Enough

Store owners on Wix had two options: use a third-party reviews app from the App Market, or go without. Both were bad. Third-party apps were hard to integrate, offered limited customization, and never truly matched a site's design.

"We want a product reviews application that has the look and feel of Wix."

Direct user feedback from Wix community

Users weren't just complaining about aesthetics. The friction of setting up external tools meant many store owners simply didn't collect reviews at all - leaving a huge trust signal off the table.

Key Design Decisions

Four Things That Were Actually Difficult

Challenge 01

Every store looks different. Generic felt off-brand.

The decision

Three layers: Display, Design, Layout.

Challenge 02

Owner and buyer never share a screen. One email connects them.

The decision

Mapped both flows side-by-side. Added a Reviews tab with full visibility into Published, Pending, and Rejected.

Site Owner
Add Reviews widget → Customise the widget ··· Email - new review → Review Published / Rejected
↑ Buyer's review triggers the owner's email
Buyer
Buy a product → Receive email to add review → Login → Add review
Challenge 03

Where should it live?

App Market, or the Plugin panel on the product page?

The decision

Plugin panel - zero context switch, right when intent is highest.

Challenge 04

What about zero reviews?

A bare empty state gets removed before it's ever used.

The decision

Treat it as a call to action, not a placeholder.
